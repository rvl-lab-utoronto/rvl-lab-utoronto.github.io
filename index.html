Robot Vision and Learning Lab

Welcome

Welcome to the Robot Vision and Learning (RVL) lab. We are part of the Computer Science department at the University of Toronto, the MCS department at UTM, the UofT Robotics Institute, and Vector Institute. The group is led by Prof. Florian Shkurti, and consists of students with backgrounds in robotics, machine learning, computer vision, engineering, control theory, or physics. We develop methods that enable robots to perceive, reason, and act effectively and safely, particularly in dynamic environments and alongside humans. Application areas include robotic manipulation, chemistry/biology lab automation, and field robotics for environmental monitoring.

Research Themes

  • reasoning and learning for robotics
  • safe robot learning
  • 3D vision & generative models for simulation

Application Areas

  • robotics for chemistry and biology lab automation
  • field robotics for environmental monitoring
